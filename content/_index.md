Hi, I am Charley. I am 14 years old and I love to read and draw. In my free time I also like to play volleyball. I just graduated 8th grade and next year I am going to be a freshman at the Spartan School of Visual and Performing Arts as a digital media major. What inspired me to do this project is I have always grown up knowing and understanding the jobs that canines and other animals had in mental health because my mom is a psychiatrist. Whenever I went to a store or a mall it always upset me when I saw people claiming their pet is an ESA or a service animal when the pet clearly was not. I decided to solve this problem I would teach people the differences between the three types of mental health companions: what they do, how to act around them, and how people get them.
{{< /lead >}}

In this presentation you are going to learn about the three types of mental health companions, the differences between them, how to act around them, and what their jobs are in the real world.

Mall security came up to tell her that pets were not allowed in the mall, and she began arguing with him that it was her ESA animal and she was allowed to bring the dog around with her.
