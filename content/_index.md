Hi, I am Charley. This project is my Girl Scout silver award on Canine Mental Health companions. What inspired me to do this project is I have always grown up knowing and understanding the jobs that canines and other animals had in mental health because my mom is a psychiatrist. Whenever I went to a store or a mall it always upset me when I saw people claiming their pet is an ESA or a service animal when the pet clearly was not. I decided to solve this problem I would teach people the differences between the three types of mental health companions: what they do, how to act around them, and how people get them.
